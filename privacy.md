### Summary
Trekbok is built on a simple principle: **your travel history belongs only to you**. We do not collect, store, transmit, or monetize your personal data. Trekbok operates without user accounts, analytics trackers, advertising networks, or third-party servers.

---

### 1. Data Collection & Storage
We collect no personal data:
* **No Account Required:** You do not need to register, provide an email address, or create a password to use the app.
* **Local Storage:** All trip entries, date calculations, country logs, and preferences are stored locally on your device.
* **No Developer Access:** The developer cannot access, view, intercept, or retrieve any information you enter into Trekbok.

---

### 2. Apple iCloud Sync & Backups
If you have iCloud enabled on your device:
* Trekbok utilizes Apple’s native iCloud service to back up and synchronize your trip records across your personal Apple devices.
* This data is encrypted and stored exclusively within your private Apple iCloud account. It is governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
* Neither the developer nor any third party has access to your data through iCloud.
* You can disable iCloud backup or synchronization at any time directly through your device's iOS Settings under your Apple ID.

---

### 3. In-App Purchases
When purchasing Trekbok Platinum, transactions are processed directly by Apple through the App Store using StoreKit. We do not collect, store, or process your credit card details, billing address, or payment credentials. All payment processing is subject to Apple's terms and privacy practices.

---

### 4. Third-Party Services & Tracking
Trekbok contains:
* **No analytics SDKs** (e.g., Google Analytics, Mixpanel, Firebase).
* **No advertising frameworks**.
* **No tracking cookies, device fingerprinting, or diagnostic telemetry**.

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with data brokers, advertisers, or any third parties.

---

## 5. Children’s Privacy
Trekbok does not collect personal information from any user, including children under the age of 13.
